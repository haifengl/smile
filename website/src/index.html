Large Language Models

Deep Learning & Vision

Build, train, and deploy neural nets with Conv2d, norms, optimizers, and pretrained EfficientNet-V2.

Speed

Advanced data structures deliver state-of-the-art performance — often faster than R, Python, Spark, or H2O.

Declarative visualization

Interactive Swing plots and Vega-Lite charts in the browser.

Classic ML & NLP

Hundreds of algorithms — classification, regression, clustering, manifold learning, and classic NLP — behind a clean API.

Math & Statistics

Linear algebra, dense and sparse matrices, distributions, hypothesis tests, and descriptive statistics for ML pipelines.
